Thinking, Communication & Expression in Practice

In my critical thinking course at LIA Wuming International Academy, students begin by recognizing their feelings and thoughts. Through sorting activities, games, discussions and interviews, they practise evaluating information and understanding different perspectives, then organize and present their views in a mini debate.

THINK / LISTEN / SPEAK

Support your views with reasons.
Make space for other voices.

Debate gives students a chance to practise expression. They take turns explaining their positions, giving reasons, listening and responding, bringing inquiry, thinking and communication into a live presentation.

Explore the classroom activities ↓

How We Learn in Class

  1. Understanding ourselves

    Distinguish facts, feelings and thoughts, and recognize emotions and inner reactions.

  2. Seeing another perspective

    Use “thinking glasses” activities to explore different interpretations and responses to setbacks.

  3. Evaluating messages

    From drawing relays to spotting false information, practise verification and media literacy.

  4. Understanding others

    Compare perspectives and use role reversal to practise empathy and listening.

  5. Questions and interviews

    Ask open-ended questions, focus on one thing at a time and avoid suggesting an answer in the question.

  6. Developing and expressing a view

    Organize reasons, speak clearly and respond to different opinions through a mini debate.

ARC Parent Circle

As children grow, parents deserve understanding and support too.

A six-week facilitated journey, meeting once a week for two hours. Starting with self-awareness, parents build a steadier foundation of family support through understanding and acceptance.

ARC × RECODE

From automatic reactions to intentional parenting.

In ARC Parent Circle, we explore children’s behavior alongside the moments that trigger parents: how needs, core fears, old patterns and beliefs can drive control, anxiety or lecturing. Through RECODE, parents revisit their reactions and practise clearer, steadier ways of relating to their children.

EMOTIONAL AGILITY WORKSHOP

HOOKED | Automatic Reactions & Psychological Rigidity

3-hour core workshop · Po-Feng Hsu & Konya Chiang

Two facilitators guide case discussions and hands-on activities to help participants find space to choose a response between stimulus and reaction.

  • Notice automatic reactions:Recognize how the mind jumps to conclusions and identify habitual responses and defenses.
  • Practise reality checks:Separate observable facts from interpretations and question the stories we fill in.
  • Recognize team blind spots:Understand how personal defenses affect communication, cross-team collaboration and organizational decisions.
  • Take home everyday practices:Use brief mental pauses and grounding breaths to practise responding under pressure.
